Double-click this file:

D:\Users\Lee\Documents\GitHub\mb-realm-generator\serve.bat
It starts the local server and automatically opens http://localhost:8321/index.html in your browser — that's your full version: tile art, clickable page references with the in-page rulebook reader, and working GM/player PNG exports.

Keep the black console window it opens minimized while you use the generator (closing it stops the server). When you're done, just close that window.

If you only need a quick look at a map and don't care about exports or the rulebook popups, double-clicking index.html directly also works — but the served version is the complete experience, so serve.bat is the habit worth keeping.
